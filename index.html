04 / Method

Good geometry is a sequence of decisions, not just a final screenshot.

01

Read the reference

Understand the Class-A input, the surrounding package, the functional intent, and what the final part has to communicate.

02

Establish direction

Choose tooling direction, draft strategy, section logic, and the surface boundaries that will keep the model under control.

03

Build the hidden logic

Develop offsets, B-side surfaces, C-surfaces, attachment features, and clearance decisions behind the visible form.

04

Close, check, explain

Validate the part, convert it into a coherent solid where appropriate, and present the reasoning so another engineer can review it.
